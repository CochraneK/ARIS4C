# ARIS4C021 · Mass Atrocity Conditions · QCA

## Question

Across comparable political crises and country-years, what conditions or combinations of conditions are consistently associated with the onset of genocide, politicide, or intrastate mass killing?

The project is prevention-oriented. It studies risk configurations and resilience, not operational methods for perpetrating violence.

## Core idea

Two outputs are deliberately separated:

1. Set-theoretic inference — test necessary conditions, sufficient configurations, equifinality and causal asymmetry using csQCA/fsQCA.
2. Risk-condition atlas — a periodic-table-like visualization that groups candidate conditions by causal role and reports only evidence-supported status.

The atlas is not allowed to turn a correlation into a “law”. Every cell must link back to its outcome definition, case universe, calibration, consistency/coverage, robustness and provenance.

## Why this is not just another risk-factor regression

Barbara Harff's classic cross-national model identified six structural risk factors distinguishing state-failure episodes that did and did not lead to geno-/politicide. Timothy Williams later revisited the question with QCA over 139 genocide/non-genocide cases and reported a sufficient configuration centered on autocracy + salient elite ethnicity + (exclusionary ideology OR political upheaval).

ARIS4C021 therefore starts with replication, not novelty theater. Its contribution target is:

- reproduce classic Harff/Williams results under explicit frozen coding;
- test whether the configurations survive updated data and alternative outcome definitions;
- separate structural/priming conditions from proximate triggers;
- test the absence of atrocity as its own asymmetric outcome;
- quantify fragile vs stable necessary/sufficient claims;
- expose contradictory and deviant cases rather than hiding them;
- produce a human-readable condition atlas after, not before, inference.

## Outcome hierarchy

- Primary replication outcome: onset of PITF genocide/politicide within the relevant political-instability/state-failure case universe.
- Secondary extension outcome: onset of intrastate mass killing under the Early Warning Project definition.
- Sensitivity outcome: broader atrocity-event escalation, kept analytically separate from genocide/mass-killing onset.

Outcome definitions must never be silently pooled.

## First gate

Before inspecting any new configuration result:

1. freeze the case universe;
2. freeze outcome onset rules and temporal lag;
3. recreate the Harff/Williams core condition matrix;
4. preregister calibration anchors and QCA thresholds;
5. create the negative/control case logic;
6. hash the frozen matrix;
7. only then run necessity and sufficiency analyses.
