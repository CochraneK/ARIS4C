# ARIS4C021 · Agent takeover brief

## What this project is

**Necessary, Sufficient, and Sequential Conditions of Mass Atrocity Onset: A Multimethod Comparative Study**

A prevention-oriented multimethod comparative study of the conditions, combinations, sequences, and timing under which genocide, politicide, and intrastate mass killing begin. QCA is retained as one replication and configurational tool, alongside Coincidence Analysis (CNA), Necessary Condition Analysis (NCA), event-history/rare-event models, process tracing, sequence-sensitive comparison, and predictive benchmarks. A periodic-table-like condition atlas is an evidence-synthesis layer rather than a deterministic causal recipe.
