# ARIS4C021 · Decisions

## 2026-09-24 · Project created

- Frame the project as mass-atrocity explanation and prevention, not a perpetration recipe.
- Use QCA/set-theoretic language precisely: necessity and sufficiency are case-universe/calibration dependent.
- Harff 2003 is the structural-risk replication anchor.
- Williams 2016 is the direct QCA replication anchor.
- Primary replication outcome is PITF genocide/politicide onset within the appropriate political-instability universe.
- Early Warning Project intrastate mass killing is a separate secondary outcome, not silently pooled with genocide/politicide.
- First six-condition replication core: prior genocide/politicide, political upheaval, exclusionary ideology, autocracy, elite ethnic character/salience, low trade openness.
- Modern extension variables remain locked until historical replication is completed.
- Necessary-condition analysis precedes sufficiency analysis.
- Analyze outcome absence separately to test causal asymmetry and resilience.
- The periodic-table-like visualization is downstream of inference and may only display evidence-supported roles plus uncertainty.
