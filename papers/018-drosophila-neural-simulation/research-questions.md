# ARIS4C018 · Research Question Pool

Status: idea pool, not hypotheses.

## Computational neuroscience questions

### 1. Circuit simplicity and behavioural complexity

Question:

Can a relatively small biological circuit generate stable behaviour through architecture alone?

Potential system:

- central complex navigation

Needed:

- connectome
- circuit model
- behavioural output

---

### 2. Biological robustness

Question:

Why can biological circuits tolerate missing/noisy components?

Potential approach:

- perturb simulated neurons
- compare behavioural degradation

---

### 3. Biology and AI comparison

Question:

Which principles of biological circuits are absent from artificial neural networks?

Possible comparison:

- sparse connectivity
- recurrent motifs
- energy constraints
- robustness

## Selection criteria

A question becomes a dedicated project only when:

- a clear hypothesis exists;
- data/model source is available;
- simulation pipeline is reproducible;
- expected result can be falsified.
