# Literature Positioning · 2026-09-24

This is a working novelty boundary, not a completed systematic review.

## Closest recent studies

### Koo & Lin · Heliyon 2024
**Retracted articles in scientific literature: A bibliometric analysis from 2003 to 2022 using the Web of Science**
- 8,466 retracted articles in Web of Science;
- broad country/journal/reason description;
- restricted to WoS SCI-expanded records rather than the complete RWDB population.
- DOI: 10.1016/j.heliyon.2024.e38620

### Lendvai & Sasvári · Journal of Information Science 2026
**‘Wasted’ research and lost citations: A scientometric assessment of retracted documents in Scopus between 2001 and 2024**
- 35,514 Scopus retracted publications;
- temporal surges and citation dynamics;
- large-scale but Scopus-defined population.

### Cheng, Yang & Allen · Scientometrics 2026
**Tracking the retracted paper mill articles: a bibliometric study**
- 10,409 paper-mill retractions through 2024;
- demonstrates that 2023 is strongly affected by concentrated paper-mill activity.
- DOI: 10.1007/s11192-026-05751-6

### Mañana-Rodríguez, Granadino-Goenechea & Bautista-Puig · Scientometrics 2026
**An analysis of the relationship between access models and retractions with peer review issues (2014–2024)**
- matched 31,910 retracted publications between OpenAlex and RWDB;
- explicitly estimated retraction rates using publication denominators;
- establishes a strong precedent for denominator-aware RWDB × OpenAlex research.
- DOI: 10.1007/s11192-026-05668-0

### García-Romero et al. · Scientometrics 2026
**The enduring influence of retracted research: determinants and citation patterns across three disciplines**
- 531 papers across Business, Social Sciences and Technology;
- self-controlled pre/post citation design with negative-binomial modeling;
- directly motivates an all-field extension.
- DOI: 10.1007/s11192-026-05699-7

### AI-specific systematic bibliometric review · 2025/2026
- 335 retracted AI publications;
- median publication-to-retraction interval ~550 days;
- useful domain-specific comparator, not a full-database denominator study.

### Chinese retraction-lag study · 2025/2026
- 32,321 Chinese retracted papers from RWDB;
- reports median lag 378 days and heterogeneity by discipline/publisher/type/reason;
- geographically restricted.

## Working contribution target

ARIS4C-020 should not claim novelty from “using Retraction Watch” or “doing global bibliometrics.”

The defensible integrated target is the combination of:

1. complete current RWDB Retraction-event spine;
2. explicit event-vs-work deduplication;
3. all-field publication denominators;
4. cohort/right-censoring-aware time-to-retraction;
5. native 110-label multi-label reason ecology plus orthogonal facets;
6. mass-retraction / paper-mill decomposition;
7. full vs fractional country exposure;
8. all-field citation afterlife with matched controls;
9. versioned source snapshots and reproducible sensitivity gates.

The novelty claim remains provisional until a formal systematic literature search is completed.
