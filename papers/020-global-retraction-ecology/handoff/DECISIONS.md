# Decisions

## 2026-09-24 · Use full RWDB bulk data
Use Crossref's official Retraction Watch GitLab CSV as the event spine rather than scraping the Retraction Watch web UI.

## 2026-09-24 · Retraction is the primary population
Corrections and expressions of concern are exploratory only because RWDB does not claim comprehensive coverage for them.

## 2026-09-24 · Counts are not rates
No field/country/institution/journal/publisher risk comparison is allowed without an explicit eligible-publication denominator.

## 2026-09-24 · Preserve multi-label reasons
Retraction reasons remain multi-label. Any higher-order reason taxonomy must be frozen and auditable.

## 2026-09-24 · No intent inference
Retraction, affiliation, reason labels and citations are not automatically interpreted as misconduct, responsibility, or endorsement.
