# ARIS4C-019 · Annual Leave × Life Evaluation

**Working title:** Statutory Paid Annual Leave and National Life Evaluation: A Global Legal-Event Audit and Falsification-First Holdout Study

ARIS4C019 asks whether legally verified changes in statutory paid annual leave are followed by stable changes in annual population Life Ladder **within countries over time**.

The project explicitly rejects a static country ranking of “vacation days vs happiness.” It also treats legal treatment isolation as a prerequisite for causal estimation.

## Current empirical state

- Original frozen eight-event panel: useful as a broader labour/time-off reform stress test, **not** a clean annual-leave-specific causal panel.
- WHR2024 annual source refresh through 2023: mean +0.088, median -0.099, 6/8 event means negative.
- Removing Bahrain from the refreshed panel: mean -0.117.
- Bounded audit of 11 remaining World Bank leave jumps: **0 new clean holdouts**.
- Modern WORLD 2015/16 → Equal Futures 2026 legal screen identified a cleaner **Israel 2016 Annual Leave Law** holdout.
- Israel strict-115 frozen result using 2015 reference: full-post mean **+0.002**.
- Israel reference diagnostics are materially baseline-sensitive; therefore the result is **not** interpreted as a precise causal zero.

## Construct boundary

Keep separate:

1. statutory paid annual-leave entitlement;
2. statutory public holidays;
3. actual annual / weekly hours worked;
4. actual leave utilization.

The current paper focuses on **(1)**. The others are mechanism/triangulation layers and must not be silently substituted.

## Claim boundary

The strongest defensible conclusion is:

> Current reproducible annual Life Ladder evidence does not show a robust, directionally stable population response to statutory annual-leave reforms.

This does **not** imply that actual vacations or paid leave lack worker-level well-being benefits.
