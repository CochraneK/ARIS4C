## Historical bridge

The prewar Huberman–Minns segment contains **14 countries** beginning in 1870. All **14/14** appear in the materialized WDH long-run mean-variable registry.

This creates a coherent historical bridge:
- prewar actual working-time history (1870–1938);
- postwar WDH long-run subjective-well-being series (from 1946/47 onward for the earliest countries);
- later PWT/OWID working-hours observations;
- modern annual WHR Life Ladder.

The bridge does not mean the 1870–1938 hours values can be regressed against happiness that did not yet exist. It means the same country set can be followed across institutional/time-use history and postwar well-being without pretending there was a contemporaneous happiness survey in 1870.
