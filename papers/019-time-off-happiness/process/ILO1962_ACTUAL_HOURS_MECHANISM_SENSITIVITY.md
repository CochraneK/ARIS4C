## Interpretation

The direction remains weak/non-monotonic under the stricter legal-basis and exact-year restrictions. A statutory vacation floor is therefore **not a sufficient proxy for realized annual work time** in this historical cross-section.

This is substantively important for 019:
- legal entitlement is one institutional input;
- realized annual hours are a behavioral/economic outcome;
- national happiness can respond to many channels besides either.

The project should not merge statutory leave and actual work hours into one latent “time off” exposure without an explicit measurement model.
