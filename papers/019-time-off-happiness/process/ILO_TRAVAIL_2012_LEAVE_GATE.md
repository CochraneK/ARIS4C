## Harmonized legal measure

ILO defines the annual-leave field as the **minimum annual leave period mandated for a worker in the first year of service**, converted to rounded working days on an assumed **five-day workweek**. Seniority increments are not incorporated. Calendar/consecutive-day provisions are converted at 5/7; provisions expressed as working days are normalized from a presumed six-day workweek unless the law specifies otherwise. citeturn516255view0turn516255view1

This gives 019 a directly comparable legal measure that is conceptually closer to statutory entitlement than actual-hours data and broader than the prior WB event subset.

## Data-quality rule

- `no_universal_minimum` is **not 0 days**; it denotes absence of a universal national statutory floor.
- Australia is coded at the general minimum 20 days; the source separately notes 24 days for shift workers.
- Canada reflects the federal provision in the source.
- Tanzania reflects mainland Tanzania.
- Ivory Coast is retained as interval 20–23 rather than an invented exact value.
- Country labels remain close to the source; crosswalks should normalize names without rewriting source truth.
