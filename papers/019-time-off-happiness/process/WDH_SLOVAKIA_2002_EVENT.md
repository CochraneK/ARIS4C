# ARIS4C019 · Slovakia 2002 statutory-leave historical WDH event sensitivity

**Frozen event before outcome look:** Act 311/2001 effective 1 Apr 2002; basic statutory annual leave increased from 3 weeks to at least 4 weeks.  
**Outcome:** WDH 121C four-step verbal LifeSatisfaction, transformed to 0–10.  
**Status:** historical donor-adjusted sensitivity, not a clean causal estimate.

## Outcome design

- reference year: **2001**
- transition year excluded: **2002**
- main post window: **2003–2005**
- fixed donor pool: Czech Republic, Hungary, Poland, Slovenia, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania
- aggregation: average multiple WDH findings within country-year, then equal-weight countries in the donor mean
- selection was frozen without looking at the outcome values.

The WDH page provides Slovakia from 2001 and matching country-year observations for all seven donors. The regional series also share a survey-measure transition around 2004, which the donor comparison helps absorb.

## Main result

- Slovakia 2001: **5.100**
- equal-weight donor mean 2001: **5.507**
- Slovakia mean 2003–2005: **5.328**
- donor mean 2003–2005: **5.712**
- donor-adjusted before/after change: **+0.024 LifeSatisfaction points**

## Timing sensitivity

- 2003 only: **-0.081**
- 2003–2004: **-0.027**
- 2003–2005: **+0.024**

Leave-one-donor-out range for the main post window:
**-0.033 to +0.103**.

A simple donor-placebo absolute-rank diagnostic gives **1.000**; the treated change is not unusually large relative to donor changes.

## Interpretation lock

> The Slovakia 2002 reform does not show a stable positive or negative national LifeSatisfaction shift in the reconstructed WDH 121C window. The main donor-adjusted estimate is essentially zero and changes sign under short-window and leave-one-donor sensitivities.

Important limitations:
- only one clean pre-reform WDH year (2001);
- 2002 is a transition year;
- 2004 EU accession and broader transition-era changes remain possible confounders, even though the donor pool intentionally consists of the same accession cohort;
- country means are ecological outcomes, not worker-level vacation effects.

This result supports the project's broader “no stable aggregate one-direction effect” interpretation; it does not imply the reform had no individual or labor-market effects.
