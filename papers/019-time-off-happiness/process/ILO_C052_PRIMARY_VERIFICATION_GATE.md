## Convention baseline

C052 — Holidays with Pay Convention, 1936:

- adopted: **24 Jun 1936**
- entered into force: **22 Sep 1939**
- current NORMLEX register: **54 ratifications**
- currently reported as denounced/not in force after revision: **17**

Article 2 established an early international minimum:
- after one year of continuous service: at least **6 working days** of paid annual holiday;
- workers/apprentices under 16: at least **12 working days**;
- public/customary holidays and sickness interruptions are not counted inside that annual holiday.

This historical minimum is an institutional benchmark, not a claim that every country immediately implemented six days in domestic law.

## Evidence rule

- Exact ratification dates/status come only from NORMLEX.
- The project does **not** treat C052 ratification date as the first domestic paid-leave law date.
- Automatic denunciation by C132 is retained as institutional succession metadata.
- Remaining C052 rows stay pending until exact primary dates are fetched; no secondary table is allowed to fill exact dates silently.
