# ARIS4C019 · Israel Reference-Year and Concurrent-Shock Audit v0.1

**Status:** post-outcome fragility diagnostic.  
**Primary holdout estimate remains the pre-frozen 2015-reference specification. Nothing in this file changes the frozen estimand.**

## Why this audit exists

The frozen event-time analysis normalizes to the last fully untreated year, 2015. Israel's WHR Life Ladder is lower in 2015 than in 2013–2014, so a last-pre-year design could mechanically make later years look like a rebound if 2015 was temporarily depressed.

The issue is especially relevant because official Bank of Israel material documents:

- a wave of violent/terrorist incidents beginning in Q4 2015;
- almost no contemporaneous slowdown in broad domestic macroeconomic activity from that wave;
- a separate 2014 security conflict that the Bank estimated reduced 2014 GDP growth by about 0.3 percentage points.

Therefore neither 2014 nor 2015 should be post-hoc declared a uniquely “clean” reference year. Reference sensitivity is diagnostic only.

## Interpretation

- The pre-frozen 2015-reference estimate remains approximately zero.
- The sign and magnitude are **reference-sensitive** when earlier pre years or multi-year pre means are substituted after the fact.
- That sensitivity is not evidence for a positive or negative leave effect; it is evidence that the annual national outcome has material year-to-year noise relative to the modest legal treatment.
- Because both 2014 and late 2015 had documented security disturbances, selecting one alternative year after viewing outcomes would create a serious researcher-degrees-of-freedom problem.
- The appropriate conclusion is therefore narrower than a clean “null causal effect” claim: the Israel holdout shows **no stable aggregate shift that is robust to reasonable baseline diagnostics**.
