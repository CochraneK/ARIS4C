## Interpretation boundary

Ratification dates are **international institutional events**, not automatically:
- the country's first domestic statutory paid-leave law;
- the date the entitlement changed for workers;
- a causal treatment date for happiness.

They are appropriate for:
1. historical diffusion/descriptive analysis;
2. institutional-era stratification;
3. identifying candidate national legal histories for primary-law reconstruction.

They are not substitutes for national effective-law dates in event studies.
