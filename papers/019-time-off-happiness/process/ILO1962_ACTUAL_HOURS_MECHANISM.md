## Interpretation

This is a small historical overlap, not a causal estimate. Statutory leave is only one component of annual working time; weekly hours, overtime, sector mix, unemployment, part-time work and enforcement also matter.

If longer statutory leave aligns with lower actual annual hours in this overlap, that supports the mechanism's face validity but does not show that the leave law alone caused the hours difference. If the relation is weak, it reinforces the need to keep legal entitlement and realized work time separate.
