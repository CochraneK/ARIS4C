## Rejected / non-reform discrepancies

### Nicaragua
Rejected as source conflict. The apparent 2000→2012 decline is inconsistent with primary/NORMLEX legal documentation.

### Algeria
**No reform accepted.** NORMLEX states that Algerian law provides 2.5 leave days per month, capped at **30 calendar days/year**. Thirty calendar days standardize to roughly 21 five-day-workweek days, matching the 2012 ILO value. The earlier 25-day approximation was a unit-interpretation error. citeturn617828search3turn617828search5

### Mali
**No reform accepted.** CEACR states that Labour Code §L.151 grants **30 days/year while counting non-working/public/customary holidays inside that period**. This is consistent with a roughly 21-day five-day-workweek standardized value. The earlier 25-day approximation is superseded. citeturn115033search5
