# ARIS4C019 · Context

The user proposed studying changes in vacation/time-off duration across countries and their relationship with happiness.

The scientifically stronger version treats policy and working-time changes as longitudinal exposures. Cross-country levels are highly confounded by development, institutions, culture and welfare-state structure, so simple rankings/correlations are descriptive only.

Relevant prior evidence is mixed enough to justify a new synthesis/design: some legislated or organizational working-time reductions show well-being improvements, while other policy studies find weaker or null effects on life satisfaction. The project should therefore be falsification-first and allow heterogeneous/null results.
