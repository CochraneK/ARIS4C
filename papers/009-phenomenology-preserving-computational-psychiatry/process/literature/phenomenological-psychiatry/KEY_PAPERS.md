> **编制日期**：2026-09-07

## Part I: 奠基经典（Foundational Works）

### I.1 普通精神病理学

1. Jaspers, K. (1913/1963). *General Psychopathology* (7th ed., J. Hoenig & M. W. Hamilton, Trans.). Manchester University Press.  
   *—— 现象学精神病学的奠基之作。定义精神病理学为对患者主观经验的系统研究。引入静态理解、发生理解、Erklären vs. Verstehen 的框架。*
2. Jaspers, K. (1912). Die phänomenologische Forschungsrichtung in der Psychopathologie. *Zeitschrift für die gesamte Neurologie und Psychiatrie*, 9(1), 391–408.  
   *—— 精神病理学中现象学研究方向的首篇纲领性论文。*

### I.2 精神分裂症的现象学分析

1. Minkowski, E. (1927/1970). *La Schizophrénie: Psychopathologie des Schizoïdes et des Schizophrènes*. Payot.  
   *—— 提出"与现实的生成性接触丧失"（loss of vital contact with reality）作为精神分裂症核心特征。*
2. Blankenburg, W. (1971). *Der Verlust der natürlichen Selbstverständlichkeit: Ein Beitrag zur Psychopathologie symptomarmer Schizophrenien*. Enke.  
   *—— "自然明证性丧失"概念的经典来源。*
3. Binswanger, L. (1963). *Being-in-the-World: Selected Papers of Ludwig Binswanger* (J. Needleman, Trans.). Basic Books.  
   *—— 此在分析（Daseinsanalyse）在精神分裂症中的应用。*
4. Straus, E. W. (1963). *The Primary World of Senses: A Vindication of Sensory Experience* (J. Needleman, Trans.). Free Press.  
   *—— 感觉学（esthesiology）方法应用于精神状态分析。*

## Part II: Ipseity / 自我障碍模型（Ipseity-Disturbance & Self-Disorder）


### II.1 核心理论论文

1. Sass, L. A. (1992). *Madness and Modernism: Insanity in the Light of Modern Art, Literature, and Thought*. Basic Books.  
   *—— 将精神分裂症经验与 20 世纪现代主义文化进行结构对照分析。*
2. Sass, L. A., & Parnas, J. (2003). Schizophrenia, Consciousness, and the Self. *Schizophrenia Bulletin*, 29(3), 427–444.  
   *—— 提出 ipseity-disturbance model 的里程碑论文。核心三元素：hyperreflexivity, diminished self-affection, disturbed grip. **引用量极大**。*
3. Parnas, J., Sass, L. A., & Zahavi, D. (2013). Rediscovering Psychopathology: The Epistemology and Phenomenology of the Psychiatric Object. *Schizophrenia Bulletin*, 39(2), 270–277.  
   *—— 现象学精神病理学的认识论辩护：精神障碍的"对象"是经验，而非脑。*
4. Sass, L. A. et al. (2018). Varieties of Self Disorder: A Bio-Pheno-Social Model of Schizophrenia. *Schizophrenia Bulletin*, 44(4), 720–727.  
   *—— 生物-现象-社会模型：将 ipseity-disturbance 与外源因素整合。*
5. **Feyaerts, J., & Sass, L. (2024)**. Self-Disorder in Schizophrenia: A Revised View (1. Comprehensive Review–Dualities of Self- and World-Experience). *Schizophrenia Bulletin*, 50(2), 460–471.  
   *—— 2024 年最新修订：承认自我在场增强现象，将 hyperreflexivity 定位为症状的贯穿性生成因子。*
6. **Feyaerts, J., & Sass, L. (2024)**. Self-Disorder in Schizophrenia: A Revised View (2. Theoretical Revision–Hyperreflexivity as the Unifying Factor). *Schizophrenia Bulletin*, 50(2), 472–480.  
   *—— 理论修订篇：hyperreflexivity 作为核心统一性因素。*

### II.2 EASE 量表与实证证据

1. Parnas, J. et al. (2005). EASE: Examination of Anomalous Self-Experience. *Psychopathology*, 38(5), 236–258.  
   *—— 自我障碍综合评定量表的制定。包含 5 个领域、57 个条目。*
2. Raballo, A. et al. (2021). The Self in the Spectrum: A Meta-analysis of the Evidence Linking Basic Self-Disorders and Schizophrenia. *Schizophrenia Bulletin*, 47(4), 1007–1017.  
   *—— 元分析确认自我障碍特异性聚集于精神分裂症谱系（而非其他精神障碍）。*
3. Nelson, B. et al. (2019). Testing a Neurophenomenological Model of Basic Self Disturbance in Early Psychosis. *World Psychiatry*, 18(1), 104–105.  
   *—— 在早期精神病中检验自我障碍的神经现象学模型。*
4. Henriksen, M. G., & Parnas, J. (2012). Clinical Manifestations of Self-Disorders and the Gestalt of Schizophrenia. *Schizophrenia Bulletin*, 38(4), 657–660.  
   *—— 自我障碍作为精神分裂症格式塔（Gestalt）的临床操作化。*
5. Nordgaard, J., & Parnas, J. (2014). Self-Disorders and the Schizophrenia Spectrum: A Study of 100 First-Hospitalization Admissions. *Schizophrenia Bulletin*, 40(6), 1300–1307.  
   *—— 大规模实证研究确认自我障碍与精神分裂症谱系的特异性关联。*
