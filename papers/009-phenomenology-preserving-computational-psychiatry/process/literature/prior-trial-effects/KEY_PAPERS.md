# 关键论文清单：Prior Trial Effects on Working Memory

> 研究方向：精神病谱系中序列依赖、冲突适应与工作记忆的前次试次效应
> 整理日期：2026-09-07

---

## 一、核心目标论文

### [P1] Bansal, S., Bae, G.-Y., Barch, D. M., Erickson, M., Carter, C. S., MacDonald III, A. W., Luck, S. J., & Gold, J. M. (2026). Prior Trial Effects on Working Memory in Schizophrenia, Bipolar Disorder, and Healthy Control Individuals. *JAMA Psychiatry*, 83(6), 630-639.

- **DOI**: [10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2026.0196](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2026.0196)
- **PMC**: [PMC13084556](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13084556/)
- **全文**: JAMA Network / PubMed Central (Open Access)
- **核心价值**: 首次系统比较PSZ、PBD和HCS在工作记忆中前次试次效应的质的差异；提出序列偏差作为精神疾病生物标志物
- **关键数据**: HCS吸引偏差（+1.82）vs. PSZ排斥偏差（-1.99）vs. PBD中间模式（+0.44）；HCS和PSZ的偏差方向无重叠

---

## 二、前身与机制支持论文

### [P2] Bansal, S., Bae, G.-Y., Robinson, B. M., Dutterer, J., Hahn, B., Luck, S. J., & Gold, J. M. (2023). Qualitatively Different Delay-Dependent Working Memory Distortions in People With Schizophrenia and Healthy Control Participants. *Biological Psychiatry: Cognitive Neuroscience and Neuroimaging*, 8(12), 1218-1227.

- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.bpsc.2023.07.004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bpsc.2023.07.004)
- **PMC**: [PMC10792108](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10792108/)
- **核心价值**: [P1]的直接前身，在较小样本（n=31 PSZ, n=25 HCS）中发现PSZ排斥偏差，提出短时程突触可塑性（short-term plasticity）与神经适应（neuronal adaptation）竞争假说

### [P3] Stein, H., et al. (2022). Reduced serial dependence suggests NMDA receptor dysfunction in anti-NMDAR encephalitis and schizophrenia. 

- *(被[P2]引用为参考9-11，具体卷期信息见原文献)*
- **核心价值**: 抗NMDA受体脑炎患者吸引偏差减弱，PSZ显示排斥倾向——提供NMDA-STP机制的直接临床证据

### [P4] Gold, J., Bansal, S., Robinson, B., Anticevic, A., & Luck, S. J. (2023, preprint). Opposite direction spatial working memory biases in people with schizophrenia and healthy controls. *PsyArXiv*.

- **DOI**: [10.31234/osf.io/2kdje](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/2kdje)
- **PMC**: [PMC11805664](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11805664)
- **核心价值**: 空间工作记忆范式（非朝向）中复现相反方向偏差，PSZ排斥偏差与认知能力负相关

---

## 三、序列依赖（Serial Dependence）基础文献

### [P5] Fischer, J., & Whitney, D. (2014). Serial dependence in visual perception. *Nature Neuroscience*, 17, 738-743.

- **DOI**: [10.1038/nn.3689](https://doi.org/10.1038/nn.3689)
- **核心价值**: **开创性论文**——首次描述视觉序列依赖现象，提出"知觉稳定性"功能假说（perceptual continuity/stability）

### [P6] Kiyonaga, A., Scimeca, J. M., Bliss, D. P., & Whitney, D. (2017). Serial Dependence across Perception, Attention, and Memory. *Trends in Cognitive Sciences*, 21(7), 493-497.

- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.tics.2017.03.007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2017.03.007)
- **核心价值**: 序列依赖现象的领域综述——跨越知觉、注意和记忆的广义框架

### [P7] Bae, G.-Y., & Luck, S. J. (2019). Reactivation of Previous Experiences in a Working Memory Task. *Psychological Science*, 30(4), 587-595.

- **DOI**: [10.1177/0956797619887894](https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797619887894)
- **PMC**: [PMC6472175](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6472175/)
- **核心价值**: 使用EEG解码技术证明前一试次信息在当前试次工作记忆维持期间的神经激活——为序列依赖提供神经基础证据

### [P8] Fritsche, M., Spaak, E., & de Lange, F. P. (2020). Opposing effects of recent history on perception and decision. *Current Biology*, 30(8), 1503-1510.

- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.cub.2020.02.065](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2020.02.065)
- **核心价值**: 区分吸引性偏差（知觉水平）与排斥性偏差（决策水平）的双过程理论

### [P9] Bliss, D. P., Sun, J. J., & D'Esposito, M. (2017). Serial dependence is absent at the time of perception but increases in visual working memory. *Scientific Reports*, 7, 14739.

- **DOI**: [10.1038/s41598-017-15199-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-15199-7)
- **核心价值**: 序列依赖在感知时刻不存在，但在工作记忆延迟期间增强

## 四、冲突适应（Conflict Adaptation）与精神疾病

### [P10] Abrahamse, E., Ruitenberg, M., & Morrens, M. (2016). Conflict adaptation in schizophrenia: reviewing past and previewing future efforts. *Cognitive Neuropsychiatry*, 21(3), 197-211.

- **DOI**: [10.1080/13546805.2016.1167679](https://doi.org/10.1080/13546805.2016.1167679)
- **核心价值**: 综述冲突适应（CSE比例/后冲突减缓等）在精神分裂症中的研究现状，结论为"无明确答案"

### [P11] Hurtado, M. M., Triviño, M., Panadero, M. A., Arnedo, M., & Tudela, P. (2018). Comparing adaptation in emotional and non-emotional conflict in patients with schizophrenia and borderline personality disorder. *Neuropsychologia*, 117, 558-565.

- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2018.07.016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuropsychologia.2018.07.016)
- **PubMed**: [30025790](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30025790)
- **核心价值**: SZ显示正常的Gratton效应（冲突适应），BPD在情绪任务中Gratton效应反转——跨诊断质的差异

### [P12] Ghaneirad, E., Borgolte, A., Szycik, G. R., et al. (2026). Is conflict adaptation disrupted in schizophrenia? An ERP study. *Cognitive Neuropsychiatry*, 30(5-6), 366-379.

- **DOI**: [10.1080/13546805.2026.2639128](https://doi.org/10.1080/13546805.2026.2639128)
- **核心价值**: 使用confound-minimized Flanker任务+ERP（P2, N2, P3），发现SZ的CSE行为与神经模式均保留——与[P10]的矛盾部分呼应

### [P13] Davelaar, E. J. (2012). When the ignored gets bound: sequential effects in the flanker task. *Frontiers in Psychology*, 3, 552.

- **DOI**: [10.3389/fpsyg.2012.00552](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2012.00552)
- **PMC**: [PMC3534361](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3534361/)
- **核心价值**: 提出Flanker任务中CSE主要由侧翼刺激重复解释而非真正的冲突适应——对方法学的重要警示

## 五、情境处理（Context Processing）与认知控制

### [P14] Barch, D. M., & Ceaser, A. (2012). Cognition in schizophrenia: core psychological and neural mechanisms. *Trends in Cognitive Sciences*, 16(1), 27-34.

- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.tics.2011.11.015](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2011.11.015)
- **核心价值**: 提出精神分裂症认知缺损的"前援控制受损"（proactive control deficit）统一理论——[P1]作者Barch执笔

### [P15] Brambilla, P., MacDonald III, A. W., Sassi, R. B., et al. (2007). Context processing performance in bipolar disorder patients. *Bipolar Disorders*, 9(3), 230-237.

- **DOI**: [10.1111/j.1399-5618.2007.00398.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1399-5618.2007.00398.x)
- **核心价值**: BD患者情境处理缺损（AX-CPT）轻于SZ——[P1]多个作者参与

### [P16] Reilly, J. L., Hill, S. K., Gold, J. M., et al. (2017). Impaired Context Processing is Attributable to Global Neuropsychological Impairment in Schizophrenia and Psychotic Bipolar Disorder. *Schizophrenia Bulletin*, 43(4), 876-885.

- **DOI**: [10.1093/schbul/sbx026](https://doi.org/10.1093/schbul/sbx026)
- **PMC**: [PMC5605273](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5605273/)
- **核心价值**: B-SNIP联盟大型研究（n=1709）：情境处理缺损可用一般认知缺损解释，非特异性

### [P17] Barch, D. M., Carter, C. S., MacDonald III, A. W., Braver, T. S., & Cohen, J. D. (2003). Context-processing deficits in schizophrenia: Diagnostic specificity, 4-week course, and relationships to clinical symptoms. *Journal of Abnormal Psychology*, 112(1), 132-143.

- **DOI**: [10.1037/0021-843X.112.1.132](https://doi.org/10.1037/0021-843X.112.1.132)
- **核心价值**: AX-CPT范式在精神分裂症中的经典应用

## 七、计算与神经机制模型

### [P20] Mongillo, G., Barak, O., & Tsodyks, M. (2008). Synaptic theory of working memory. *Science*, 319(5869), 1543-1546.

- **DOI**: [10.1126/science.1150769](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1150769)
- **核心价值**: 提出"安静工作记忆"（silent WM）的突触理论——在无持续放电情况下通过短时突触可塑性维持信息

### [P21] Becker, S., Nold, A., & Tchumatchenko, T. (2020). Formation and synaptic control of active transient working memory representations. *bioRxiv*.

- **DOI**: [10.1101/2020.08.30.273995](https://doi.org/10.1101/2020.08.30.273995)
- **核心价值**: 计算模型探究精神分裂症相关突触信号失调如何影响WM维持持续时间

### [P22] Arnsten, A. F. T., Paspalas, C. D., Gamo, N. J., Yang, Y., & Wang, M. (2010). Dynamic Network Connectivity: A new form of neuroplasticity. *Trends in Cognitive Sciences*, 14(8), 365-375.

- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.tics.2010.05.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2010.05.003)
- **核心价值**: PFC网络连接的分子机制（NMDA/cAMP/HCN）——阐释精神分裂症的认知脆弱性
