# Pilot-0 Case Frame Freeze

Frozen: 2026-09-24  
N = 50 Solar-System bodies

## Composition

- 8 planets
- 5 IAU-recognized dwarf planets
- 21 natural satellites
- 16 small bodies / boundary objects

The frame is deliberately not the 50 largest objects. It mixes scale, composition, atmosphere, geology, orbital hierarchy, and dynamical regime so size cannot mechanically determine the result.

## Boundary anchors

- Mars: motivating positive-control planet.
- Ganymede, Titan, Triton, Charon, Moon: large or planet-like satellite counterexamples.
- Phobos and Deimos: small irregular satellite anchors.
- Vesta, Pallas, Hygiea and large trans-Neptunian bodies: self-gravity/shape boundaries.
- Bennu, Ryugu, Itokawa: rubble-pile small-body contrasts.
- Sedna: solar-environment extreme.

Candidate dwarf planets remain official_class = small_body unless an authoritative IAU source changes their class. boundary_tag preserves their scientific role.

Any later case-frame amendment must state why the case changed, whether QCA solutions had already been inspected, and rerun primary analyses under both the frozen and amended frames.
