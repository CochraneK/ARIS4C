## Core rule

**Surface composition is not automatically bulk composition.**  
Where spectroscopy and bulk constraints align, the body receives a substantive class. Where only surface spectra/colors or weak density constraints exist, the state is `NA_UNCERTAIN` / `NA_NOT_MEASURED` rather than an invented interior.
