# ROBUSTNESS + SENSITIVITY PLAN · v0.2

Mandatory before submission-grade claims:

## Source sensitivity
- primary bundle vs alternate bundle;
- leave-one-witness-out;
- remove fragmentary witnesses;
- remove later-comparator material.

## Translation sensitivity
- alternate scholarly translations for high-leverage passages;
- source-language locator retained.

## Ontology sensitivity
- 24-motif anchor only;
- expanded ontology;
- sibling-motif merge/split alternatives;
- remove motifs with weak inter-coder performance.

## Missingness sensitivity
- strict not_observed;
- conservative bundle absence;
- analysis restricted to high-coverage dyads.

## Metric sensitivity
- Jaccard;
- Sørensen-Dice;
- overlap coefficient;
- simple matching as a negative diagnostic.

## Case sensitivity
- leave-one-tradition-out;
- leave-one-language-family-out;
- remove known literary-dependence positive controls;
- Tier A only vs Tier A + Tier B.

## Mechanism sensitivity
- ancestry only;
- contact only;
- environment only;
- full model;
- alternate contact coding;
- topology-only vs dated-tree subset.

## Coder sensitivity
- clean external/human validation subset;
- model-family re-code subset;
- adjudicated vs unadjudicated anchor set.

A claim is “robust” only if direction/substantive interpretation survives the sensitivity family relevant to that claim.
