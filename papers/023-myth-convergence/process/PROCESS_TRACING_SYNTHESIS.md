# Process-Tracing Synthesis · v0.1

## PT01 · Mesopotamian ↔ Hebrew flood
Current evidence supports a **Mesopotamian literary-dependence family of explanations** substantially better than an inference based on motif similarity alone. Old Babylonian flood traditions are securely earlier; specialist studies argue that Genesis flood layers were modeled on earlier Mesopotamian traditions, with Atrahasis often a closer comparator than extant Gilgamesh XI. Judean residence in Babylonia supplies a historically real contact opportunity. The exact manuscript/scribe/intermediary route remains unresolved, so the project does not claim direct Genesis ← Gilgamesh copying.

## PT02 · Chinese water-catastrophe contrast
The Chinese bundle is structurally different: destructive water is present, while the selected-survivor/vessel/post-flood-survival package is largely absent from the frozen source bundle. Attempts to anchor the Yu tradition to a specific ~1920 BCE Yellow River event are contested. The current evidence therefore supports treating **broad water-disaster convergence / distinct narrative function** as a live explanation rather than assuming common transmission from the Near Eastern flood package.

## PT03 · Sumerian ↔ Akkadian anthropogony
Enki and Ninmah and Atrahasis share clay creation and a labor/service frame, and Old Babylonian scribal institutions demonstrably maintained Sumerian linguistic/literary material in an Akkadian environment. This strongly supports a **shared Mesopotamian repertoire with a plausible scribal transmission substrate**. It does not yet demonstrate direct textual dependence of Atrahasis on Enki and Ninmah specifically.

## PT04 · Vedic ↔ Greek divine conflict
The current 8-feature bundle overlap is sparse and simple matching is inflated by shared absences. However, Indo-European comparative poetics supplies independent genealogical evidence for serpent/dragon-slaying themes. This is a useful negative control: **feature-level similarity can understate genealogical continuity just as shared absences can overstate similarity**.

## Mechanism-status summary
- Vertical inheritance: supported as a real mechanism in PT04 and potentially within Mesopotamian traditions, but requires source-specific genealogy rather than language-family labels alone.
- Horizontal diffusion/contact: strongly plausible for PT01/PT03; exact route is resolved more strongly for scribal substrate than for individual textual copying.
- Environmental convergence: plausible for broad flood/water-disaster motifs, but event-to-myth mappings are too contested for a numeric causal variable at present.
- Cognitive/social convergence: remains the residual/alternative mechanism and has not yet received an independent operational test.

## What is *not* concluded
No 'single lost civilization/source' is inferred. No similarity score is interpreted as borrowing. No modern environmental proxy is substituted for ancient exposure. QCA remains off.
