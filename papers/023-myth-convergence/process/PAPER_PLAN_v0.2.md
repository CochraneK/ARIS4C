# PAPER PLAN · v0.2

## Working title

**Similarity Is Not Transmission: A Provenance-First Framework for Separating Inheritance, Diffusion, Ecology, and Convergence in Comparative Mythology**

## Article type

Primary: **Methods paper with empirical demonstration**.

Upgrade to Original Research only if:
- the primary ancient-text case universe contains at least 15 independent tradition-time units;
- at least 50 analyzable dyads exist for one or more motif families after comparability rules;
- independent validation passes;
- primary mechanism models and sensitivity analyses converge sufficiently to support empirical claims.

Otherwise the final submission remains a Methods/Perspective-style contribution with a bounded empirical demonstration.

## Core research questions

### RQ1 · Measurement
Can a source-bundle, provenance-aware motif coding system produce reproducible cross-tradition similarity estimates without turning missing evidence into absence?

### RQ2 · Mechanism separation
Among analyzable dyads, how much of positive motif overlap is associated with:
- genealogical relatedness,
- independently documented historical contact,
- geographic/ecological similarity,
- temporal distance?

### RQ3 · Family heterogeneity
Do flood, anthropogony, and divine-conflict motifs show different ancestry/contact/environment signatures?

### RQ4 · Metric validity
How sensitive are substantive conclusions to:
- source-bundle definition,
- translation choice,
- missingness rules,
- motif ontology,
- similarity metric,
- coder identity?

## Primary contribution claims

C1. A source-bundle unit is safer than timeless “civilization” coding for ancient comparative mythology.
C2. Presence-based similarity must be separated from shared-absence similarity.
C3. Myth similarity, linguistic ancestry, historical contact, and ecological context must remain separate evidence layers.
C4. A preregistered mechanism model can compare these layers without treating similarity itself as proof of borrowing.

## Explicit non-claims

- No universal common-source claim.
- No “all flood myths derive from one event.”
- No civilization similarity leaderboard.
- No causal effect from modern environmental proxies.
- No QCA unless the frozen trigger is satisfied later.
- No human inter-rater reliability claim from AI-AI agreement.
