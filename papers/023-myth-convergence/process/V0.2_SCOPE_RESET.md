# ARIS4C-023 v0.2 Scope Reset · Submission-grade ARIS Run

## Why the previous 100% was withdrawn

The v0.1.1 milestone is a successful **Pilot-0 + public delivery package**. It is not a defensible claim that the research has completed a submission-grade ARIS cycle.

The previous Finish label over-weighted:
- bilingual manuscripts,
- figures/tables,
- PDF packaging,
- continuity/handoff,
while under-weighting:
- systematic literature coverage,
- case-universe definition,
- corpus sampling and source-selection sensitivity,
- clean external validation,
- preregistered inferential analysis,
- robustness/sensitivity analysis,
- adversarial reviewer loops,
- the final paper-improvement cycle.

Nothing from v0.1.1 is deleted. It is frozen as the feasibility/pilot release.

## v0.2 article identity

**Primary route:** Methods + empirical demonstration in comparative cultural evolution / computational humanities.

The contribution is not “a universal explanation of myths.” The target contribution is a reproducible framework for separating:
1. motif-level textual resemblance,
2. vertical inheritance,
3. horizontal historical contact/diffusion,
4. ecological similarity,
5. chronology/source survival,
6. residual cognitive/social convergence.

A broader Original Research claim is permitted only if v0.2 reaches the predeclared corpus/case/model thresholds below.
