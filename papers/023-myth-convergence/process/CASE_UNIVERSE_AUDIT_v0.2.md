# CASE UNIVERSE AUDIT · v0.2 · Checkpoint 1

## Result

**Universe remains open, but the Pilot case architecture has already required three structural corrections.**

### Correction 1 · split Hurrian and Hittite
The old `HUR_HIT_LBA` composite cannot be a single ancestry node. v0.2 carries separate `HUR_LBA` and `HIT_LBA` candidates.

### Correction 2 · split early Chinese source layers
The Pilot `CHN_WS_HAN` unit mixed source layers over too broad a span. It is deprecated for v0.2 inference and replaced by:
- `CHN_YAO` — Canon of Yao / early Yu flood-control layer, chronology explicitly uncertain;
- `CHN_HUAI` — Han Huainanzi cosmology/catastrophe layer.

This prevents Han material from being back-projected into an earlier Chinese case.

### Correction 3 · add the later Vedic Manu flood
`VED_RV` cannot stand for “Indian flood mythology.” The Shatapatha Brahmana contains an explicit Manu-and-fish flood narrative with warning, ship and mountain refuge. It is therefore a separate `VED_SB` candidate time slice.

## Additional candidates

- `EGY_OK`: Old Kingdom Pyramid Texts, separated from Middle/New Kingdom Egypt.
- `AVE_EARLY`: Yima's Vara, retained with high chronology/redaction uncertainty.
- `ROM_OVD`: Ovidian Deucalion/Pyrrha, useful as a late literary-network comparator / potential positive control, not as an independent pristine tradition.
