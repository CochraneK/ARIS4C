# Coding Rules · v0.1.1

These rules operationalize the reliability decision without editing either frozen coder file.

## Global missingness rule

- not_observed = relevant evidence is outside the frozen passage scope, or a marked lacuna removes the content needed to assess the motif.
- absent = the passage substantively engages the motif family/domain and the motif element is not present.
- Passage-level absence does not prove tradition-wide absence until source coverage is explicitly closed.

## Mandatory rule amendments

1. Earth/dust and clay/mud are mutually exclusive; explicit plasticity or kneading goes to ANTH_CLAY_MUD.
2. Divine-body material and primordial-body division are mutually exclusive.
3. Breath animation requires an animating infusion into a formed body; a divine spirit component mixed into material is not enough.
4. Younger-over-older succession and kingship transfer require an effected/acclaimed/decreed transfer, not a prediction alone.
5. Divine council requires a collective body; private counsel is insufficient.
6. Primordial rebellion must be inside the frozen locator and framed as insubordination.

## Regression clarifications

- ANTH_POTTER_FORMING: generic 'formed/created' is insufficient; explicit kneading/molding/manual craft operation is required.
- DIV_FATHER_SON: the principal opposing parties must actually be father and son.
- A source-language earth term combined with explicit kneading/plastic manipulation is treated as clay/mud for this ontology.

## Frozen-source rule

The original v0.1 coder packets remain immutable. v0.1.1 changes the analysis rules and adjudication layer only.
