# Environmental Evidence Contract · v0.1

Environmental convergence is a mechanism hypothesis, not a free covariate slot.

Rules:
1. A modern climate/flood-risk value cannot stand in for ancient exposure.
2. A proposed palaeoflood event must be linked to the relevant region/time and must carry disagreement/counterevidence where present.
3. Geological evidence for a flood does not establish that a specific myth descends from that event.
4. The absence of a known palaeoflood anchor is missing evidence, not evidence of no flood.
5. No environment coefficient is estimated in Pilot-0 because current event anchors are heterogeneous and contested.

The current environmental registry is therefore evidentiary/contextual only. It prevents environmental convergence from becoming an untested catch-all explanation.
