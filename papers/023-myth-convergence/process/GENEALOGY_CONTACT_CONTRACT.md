# Genealogy + Contact Contract · v0.1

Language genealogy, documented historical contact, and narrative-transmission hypotheses are separate layers and must not be collapsed.

## Genealogy
- No arbitrary numeric phylogenetic distances are assigned.
- HUR_HIT_LBA is composite Hittite + Hurrian and is not tree-eligible until source layers are split.
- Repeated time slices in the same language/tradition are longitudinal dependence, not independent phylogenetic cases.

## Historical contact
- Contact edges require external historical support independent of motif similarity.
- confirmed means the interaction relation is supported; it does not mean a myth was transmitted.

## Transmission hypotheses
- Hypotheses are stored separately and cannot be used as outcome labels or proof of borrowing.
