# Egypt Coverage Decision · v0.1

## New Kingdom divine-conflict candidate
Papyrus Chester Beatty I preserves the New Kingdom / 20th-Dynasty Contendings of Horus and Seth as a bounded literary tale. A controller post-calibration pass gives the provisional pattern: DIV_COUNCIL present; DIV_KINGSHIP_TRANSFER present; the other six calibration divine-conflict motifs absent under v0.1.1.

Because this source was added **after** the dual-AI calibration packet, the eight controller codes are stored with `analysis_excluded=true` and the bundle remains `negative_closed=false` until an independent spot-audit is performed. Thus Egypt is now source-registered without pretending it has passed the same coding gate as the original cases.

## Egyptian anthropogony
UCL's Digital Egypt explicitly warns that Egyptian creation material is dispersed across many references and that there is no single ancient Egyptian composition narrating the creation of mankind; humans are already present in much of the surviving creation material, with some accounts deriving people from the sun-god's tears. Therefore no convenient single anthropogony bundle is manufactured for the 24-motif pilot.

## Flood family
The current Egyptian corpus is not forced into the Near Eastern exterminatory-deluge ontology. Nile inundation texts are not automatically coded as flood-survival myths.
