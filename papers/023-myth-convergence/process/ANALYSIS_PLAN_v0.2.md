# ANALYSIS PLAN · v0.2

## Stage 0 · blind data quality
- coverage by case/family/motif;
- missingness mechanism;
- coder reliability;
- source-bundle overlap;
- chronology uncertainty;
- no mechanism outcomes inspected during ontology revision.

## Stage 1 · descriptive structure
- prevalence with uncertainty;
- bundle coverage;
- Jaccard / Dice / overlap coefficient;
- MDS/clustering as visualization only;
- no civilization ranking.

## Stage 2 · ancestry layer
Global:
- same-language-continuum / same-branch / same-family indicators.

Within supported language families:
- published dated phylogenies where available;
- posterior/tree uncertainty preserved when possible.

Never fabricate a single global metric by joining unrelated families at an artificial root.

## Stage 3 · contact layer
Construct before inspecting expanded motif similarity:
- direct political incorporation;
- trade;
- migration/settlement;
- diplomatic contact;
- bilingual scribal contact;
- translation/adaptation;
- intermediary-path evidence.

Adjacency alone is not transmission.

## Stage 4 · environmental layer
Only time-matched palaeoenvironmental evidence.
Modern environment is prohibited as a proxy for ancient exposure unless a sensitivity design explicitly justifies it.

## Stage 5 · primary dyadic model
Activate only after the preregistered model gate.

Primary candidate:
- bounded similarity outcome;
- two-node multi-membership random effects;
- motif-family effect or stratification;
- ancestry, contact, temporal, geographic/environment predictors entered separately.

Primary interpretation is associational unless an identification argument exists.

## Stage 6 · network/permutation robustness
MRQAP / matrix permutation or equivalent to test whether matrix dependence drives apparent associations.

## Stage 7 · process tracing
High-information and negative-control cases are chosen from predeclared rules, not only from the largest observed Jaccards.

## QCA trigger
QCA remains OFF unless:
- a meaningful set-valued outcome exists;
- conditions are not tautological restatements of motif similarity;
- case dependence is defensible;
- calibration thresholds are external to the observed solution;
- contradiction/sensitivity analysis is planned.
