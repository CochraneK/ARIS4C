## Promotion rule

An idea receives a stable `papers/00X-*` ID only after it passes a minimum novelty and feasibility check:

1. a clearly falsifiable research question;
2. identifiable public or obtainable data, or a feasible data-collection plan;
3. a concrete analysis plan with at least one rejection criterion;
4. a prior-art review showing a defensible contribution beyond existing work;
5. enough expected value to justify a full ARIS run;
6. required ARIS reviewer-bearing gates are supported by real reviewer evidence rather than self-attestation;
7. exploratory results survive enough robustness testing to freeze the paper's framing.

Ideas may be rejected, merged, split, or remain here indefinitely without consuming paper numbers.
